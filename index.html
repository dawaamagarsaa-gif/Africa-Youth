OUR PURPOSE

Understanding Africa. Empowering Youth.

Africa has a rapidly growing young population with enormous creativity, intelligence and energy. Yet millions of young people face barriers such as unemployment, limited educational opportunities, poverty and unequal access to technology.

Our goal is to research these challenges and discover practical solutions that can help African youth turn their potential into opportunity.

THE BIG QUESTION

Why does enormous potential coexist with poverty?

Africa's future depends greatly on how effectively its young population can access education, productive work, technology, capital and opportunities.

This platform brings research, data and youth voices together to better understand the problem and explore practical solutions.
