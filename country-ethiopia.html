Youth in Ethiopia

Exploring the opportunities, challenges, skills, education and economic future of Ethiopia's young population.

ETHIOPIA AT A GLANCE

A young population with enormous potential.

Ethiopia is one of Africa's most populous countries and has a large young population. Understanding how young Ethiopians access education, employment, technology and entrepreneurship is essential for understanding the country's future.

This page is designed as a research dashboard. Statistics will be connected to reliable sources and updated as new data becomes available.
