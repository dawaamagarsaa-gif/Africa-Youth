AFRICAN YOUTH RESEARCH

Understanding the Youth Opportunity in Africa.

We study the relationship between Africa's young population, education, employment, poverty, entrepreneurship and economic development.

THE CENTRAL QUESTION

If Africa has enormous youth potential, why do so many young people struggle to find opportunity?

This question does not have one simple answer. Conditions differ significantly between countries, cities and communities.

Our research therefore looks at multiple factors rather than blaming young people themselves.

THE BIG PICTURE

Africa's Youth in Numbers

532M+

Young people

The 2026 Africa Youth Employment Outlook estimates around 532 million people aged 15–35 across Africa.

132M

Expected growth

Africa's youth population is projected to increase by about 132 million during 2020–2030.

21%+

Skills challenge

World Bank analysis reports that more than one in five youth across Africa are neither in education nor working.

Statistics should always be interpreted with their definitions, age groups, countries covered and data year.
